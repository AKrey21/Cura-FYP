Final-year BSc project · Researcher: Aaron Lee (aaronlbp7@gmail.com) · Supervisor/tutor: ______________________

What the project is

Cura is a final-year project: an AI news app that summarises the day's coverage from many outlets, labels how each source frames a story, flags stories that outlets disagree on, and delivers the result as a text feed, an audio briefing, or a one-page newspaper.

What your session involved

In July 2026 you took part in one session of up to 30 minutes: you tried the app's three formats on your own device, then filled in a short paper questionnaire (six 1–5 ratings, a ranking of the formats, and two open questions). Taking part was voluntary and you could stop at any time without giving a reason. No recordings, photos, or videos were made.

How your data is handled

  • Your questionnaire is anonymous — it carries a participant code (P01–P08), not your name. Your name appears only on this consent form, which is stored separately from the questionnaires.
  • Responses are transcribed into an anonymised spreadsheet in the project repository; aggregate results and anonymised quotes from the open questions may appear in the final project report.
  • Paper forms are kept securely by the researcher and destroyed after the project is marked.
  • You may withdraw your responses at any time before the report is submitted by contacting the researcher — they will be deleted, no questions asked.
